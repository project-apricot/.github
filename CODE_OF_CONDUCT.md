# Code of Conduct

Project Apricot has adopted the [Contributor Covenant v2.1][cc]. It applies in all project
spaces and whenever someone is representing the project in public.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, keep criticism about the work rather than the
person, and accept that maintainers may decline a contribution.

Report unacceptable behaviour to **info [at] projectapricot.dev**. Reports are handled
confidentially, and maintainers will respond as they judge appropriate — up to and including
a permanent ban from project spaces.

[cc]: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html
